# WordSmart Domain Model

## 1. Word

Purpose:
Represents a vocabulary word in the WordSmart system.

What does this entity represent?
A word along with its meaning, pronunciation, examples, synonyms, antonyms, mnemonic, audio, roots, derivatives, and other learning metadata.

---

## 2. Quiz

Purpose:
Represents an assessment used to evaluate vocabulary knowledge.

What does this entity represent?
A collection of questions, answers, explanations, and scoring rules used to test a user's understanding of words.

---

## 3. Flashcard

Purpose:
Represents a quick learning and memorization unit.

What does this entity represent?
A simplified learning card containing key information about a word, such as the word, meaning, mnemonic, or example sentence, designed for rapid review.

---

## 4. Bookmark

Purpose:
Represents a user's saved word for future study.

What does this entity represent?
A relationship between a user and a word that indicates the user wants to revisit that word later.

---

## 5. Progress

Purpose:
Represents a user's learning journey within WordSmart.

What does this entity represent?
Data that tracks learning activity, completed quizzes, mastered words, review history, accuracy, and overall vocabulary growth.

---

## 6. Story

Purpose:
Represents contextual learning content.

What does this entity represent?
A story or passage that uses vocabulary words in realistic contexts to improve comprehension and retention.

---

## 7. Root

Purpose:
Represents an etymological root shared by multiple words.

What does this entity represent?
A root, its meaning, and the collection of words derived from that root, helping users understand vocabulary through word origins and patterns.
