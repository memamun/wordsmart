# WordSmart Editorial & Copywriting Guide

This document establishes the editorial guidelines, tone of voice, allowed terms, and microcopy rules for all interface elements in WordSmart.

---

## 🎙️ Tone of Voice
To maintain a premium, reading-focused experience for adult learners, all interface copywriting must align with these core attributes:
*   **Professional:** Write with grammatical accuracy and clear syntax. Avoid colloquial slang or trends.
*   **Clear:** Deliver directions directly. Minimize word count and avoid double negatives.
*   **Academic:** Present vocabulary and definitions constructively. Encourage etymology, context exploration, and reading.
*   **Supportive:** Help the user when errors occur or study streaks are threatened. Avoid shaming or pushy notifications.

---

## 🚫 Gamification Restrictions (The Microcopy Rules)
WordSmart is designed for learning, not entertainment. The interface must remain clean and avoid childish exclamation points, gaming references, or emoji crowding:
*   **Never** use multiple consecutive exclamation marks (e.g. `Congrats!!!`).
*   **Never** use gaming terms like `Level Up!`, `Score Booster!`, or `You Rock!`.
*   **Minimize** emojis (only use streak fire `🔥`, success checkmarks `✓`, and icons in the Collections card stack).

---

## 📝 Microcopy Mappings

### 1. General Activities
*   *Yes:* **Practice**
*   *No:* Study Session, Play Quiz, Game Time
*   *Yes:* **Review Session**
*   *No:* Flashcards Deck, Test Prep

### 2. Spaced Repetition Ratings
*   *Yes:* **Review Again** (re-queues word)
*   *No:* Hard, Forgot It, Oops
*   *Yes:* **Good** (normal spacing)
*   *No:* Medium, OK
*   *Yes:* **Mark Mastered** (moves to easy)
*   *No:* Easy, Mastered!

### 3. Study Metrics & Streaks
*   *Yes:* **8 of 20 completed**
*   *No:* 8/20, Progress: 40% Complete!!
*   *Yes:* **Keep your learning streak alive.**
*   *No:* Don't lose your fire! Streak on!
*   *Yes:* **12-day streak • 412 words mastered**
*   *No:* Streak: 12 days, Words: 412 learned!

### 4. Search Empty Fallbacks
*   *Yes:* **Discover**
*   *No:* Popular Searches, What's Hot, Trending Words
*   *Yes:* **No matches found. Check spelling.**
*   *No:* Oops! No results! Try again.

### 5. Dialog Actions
*   *Yes:* **Delete** / **Reset** / **Clear**
*   *No:* Yes, please / Delete it now!
*   *Yes:* **Cancel**
*   *No:* Go Back / Keep it
